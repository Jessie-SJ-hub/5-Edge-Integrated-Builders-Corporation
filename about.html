WHO WE ARE

One team.
Integrated solutions.

5 Edge Integrated Builders Corporation is a Cebu-based engineering and construction company providing integrated solutions for residential, commercial, and industrial developments.

We bring together engineering expertise, building construction, specialized building systems, and regulatory compliance under one coordinated team.

Our multidisciplinary approach allows us to support projects from planning and design, permitting and procurement, through construction, installation, testing and commissioning, and project completion.

With a commitment to quality, safety, integrity, and technical excellence, 5 Edge delivers practical and reliable solutions tailored to the needs of every project.
